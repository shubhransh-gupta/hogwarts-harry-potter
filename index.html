Hogwarts Express

Seven enchanted chapters connecting Lucknow Charbagh Station to the ancient towers of Hogwarts hidden in the Himalayan foothills of Dehradun. Pick a realm.

DISCLAIMER This game is created purely for Harry Potter fans, exploration, and fun in the browser. All train journeys, spells, and magical creatures are playful tributes—have fun exploring!

Walk with WASD and the mouse. Cast spells with Q-Z & Left-Click. E to talk / mount broom, 1-8 to spawn. Sound on.

Explore Hogwarts grounds freely with your wand. Best experienced with headphones.

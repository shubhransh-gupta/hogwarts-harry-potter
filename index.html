Hogwarts Dehradun: The Lucknow 9¾ Express

Tucked secretly between Platforms 9 and 10 of the majestic red-brick arches of Lucknow Charbagh Railway Station lies the enchanted brick barrier to Platform 9¾. Beyond the solid masonry, steam billows from the scarlet locomotive: the iconic 5972 Hogwarts Express, bound across the Shivalik foothills for Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry—hidden high within the mist-crowned pine mountains of Dehradun.

Hold your wand in hand, cast ancient spells (Lumos, Expecto Patronum, Wingardium Leviosa, Incendio), explore the Great Hall's floating candles, the soaring Astronomy Tower, the Quidditch pitch, Hagrid's hut, and the Forbidden Himalayan Forest.
